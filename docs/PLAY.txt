HAUNTER IN THE DARK - v0.4.0
Windows 64-bit prototype

GET STARTED
1. Extract the entire ZIP using Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder and run HaunterInTheDark.exe.
3. Press Enter to begin. Headphones help locate sounds.

Unity is not needed to play. Keep the EXE beside HaunterInTheDark_Data,
UnityPlayer.dll, MonoBleedingEdge, and all other included files. Do not
run it from inside the ZIP or move the EXE by itself.

YOUR OBJECTIVE
Reach the exit door while avoiding the creature guarding it. Follow the
arrow at the top of the screen. Sound sends ripples through the room,
briefly revealing white outlines. The creature's pawsteps reveal it too.

The creature is faster than your sprint. Crouch to reduce footstep noise.
Pick up a nearby object and throw it away from your escape route. An
impact close enough for the creature to hear can interrupt its pursuit.
It commits to that impact, travels there, then investigates for five full
seconds before returning to the exit. New sounds cannot redirect it or
extend that investigation. Use the opening to sneak past, but avoid
touching the creature even while it is distracted.

GUIDED OPENING
The new tutorial teaches you during the encounter: tap the cane, watch
the creature by the exit, and take two steps. When it charges, time
freezes. Press E to pick up the highlighted object, then hold and release
left mouse to throw where the camera aims. Watch it investigate, then
hold Ctrl or C and move quietly as it returns to the exit.

The camera and movement are locked during the pickup and throw lessons.
Take as long as you need; pausing preserves the frozen step. The tutorial
restarts with each new run, including after death. Press T to skip it, or
use Play without tutorial on the title screen / Skip tutorial & resume
on the pause screen. The character's reaction is shown as a caption.

CONTROLS
WASD / mouse              Move / look
Space or right mouse      Tap your cane
Ctrl or C                 Crouch and move quietly
Shift                     Sprint with louder footsteps
E                         Pick up an object / use the exit
Hold/release left mouse   Charge and throw the held object
Q                         Drop the held object
Esc                       Pause / resume and release the cursor
R after an ending         Restart
T                         Skip the guided opening
Alt+F4                    Close the game

This is a single-room prototype with primitive art and procedural sound.
This download is for Windows desktop, not a browser, macOS, or Linux.

PLAY IN A BROWSER
https://graysonwills.github.io/haunter-in-the-dark/
Use a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse. No installation needed.
Browser controls differ: hold Shift or C to crouch, and hold R to sprint.
Shift + W moves forward quietly. The Windows controls above are unchanged.

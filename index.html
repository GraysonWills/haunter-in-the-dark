Haunter in the Dark

An echolocation horror prototype

Sound is your only sight.

Feel the room through echoes. Draw the creature away.
Find your way out of the dark.

Preparing the game…

Designed for a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse.

Keyboard + mouse · Headphones recommended · No installation needed

Reach the exit

Every footstep and cane tap sends a ripple through the room, revealing its edges. The creature hears you too. Throw an object nearby to lure it away from the door. Once it reaches the sound, you have five seconds to slip past. Its pawsteps show you where it is.
